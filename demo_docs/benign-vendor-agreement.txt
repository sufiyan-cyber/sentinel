VENDOR SERVICE AGREEMENT — SUMMARY SHEET
Blue Sparrow Technologies  /  Northwind Cloud Services
Reference: VSA-2026-0417

1. PARTIES
   Blue Sparrow Technologies ("Client") and Northwind Cloud Services
   ("Provider") enter this agreement effective 1 April 2026.

2. SERVICES
   Provider will host the Client's analytics workloads on dedicated compute,
   with 99.9% monthly uptime and 24x7 support for severity-1 incidents.

3. TERM
   Initial term of twelve (12) months, auto-renewing for successive twelve
   month terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice.

4. FEES
   USD 4,200 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears. Late payments accrue
   interest at 1.0% per month.

5. DATA
   Provider processes Client data solely to deliver the services. Data is
   encrypted at rest and in transit. Provider will not access Client data
   except as required for support, and only with a logged, time-boxed grant.

6. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty
   (30) days of written notice. On termination Provider returns or deletes
   all Client data within fifteen (15) days.

7. LIABILITY
   Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve
   months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or
   consequential damages.

8. OPEN ITEMS
   - Security addendum still under legal review.
   - Data-residency region to be confirmed (EU vs US).
   - Named support contacts to be exchanged after signature.
